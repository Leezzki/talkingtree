Scientists Say Trees Can Send Text Messages!

Scientists say they have created a new way for trees to send messages to people. Special sensors are placed on the tree to check if it needs water.

The tree can then choose a message and send it to a phone!
A tree sends a message after its sensor checks the soil.

📱 What do the trees say?

Scientists say trees can choose from different messages.

“I am thirsty! Please give me some water! 🌳💧”
“It is too hot today. I need some shade! ☀️”

🌳 Amazing tree facts

💬

50 Messages

Trees can learn up to 50 different messages.

📲

Phone Alerts

The messages can be sent straight to a mobile phone.

🌳🌳

Tree to Tree

Some trees can even send messages to other trees.

Dr. Holly Wood checks a tree sensor.
Scientists say trees may be able to message each other.
“Trees have lots to say. We just needed to learn how to listen!” — Dr. Holly Wood, Tree Communication Scientist

🏫 Could your school tree text you?

Scientists hope the technology will be used in schools next year.

Your school could soon receive messages from trees in the playground!
